# MysteryMatrix

## Introduction
While Virtual Reality (VR) is often associated with education, training, and productivity, its potential as a tool for immersive entertainment and social engagement remains underutilized and can seamlessly integrate into daily life as a source of recreation and creative problem-solving. 

The Mystery Matrix VR escape room offers a thrilling and immersive experience that brings the excitement of puzzle-solving to life. Set in a virtual environment, it provides players with cognitive challenges in an engaging and interactive environment

Set in the 1900s, the plot revolves around a dangerous serial killer who has locked our main character and a friend inside their lair. We must solve puzzles and unlock ourselves before the killer comes back . The sense of urgency enhances our experience of the game and spontaneous problem solving skills. Every time we solve a clue ,we will be lead to the next until we’re done.

## Design ideas
- The virtual environment features life-like assets that mirror the appearance and feel of a real escape room. Every detail, from furniture to hidden objects, is crafted to create a sense of immersion like players are in a physical game
- Animated Companion Character: To enhance interactivity, the game introduces an animated character who acts according to the storyline of the simulation. Since out game is 1-player it will help players feel a sense of connection and engagement
- Interaction Mechanics: User-friendly controls and intuitive interactions allow players to naturally interact with objects, manipulate puzzles, and navigate the room. Gestures and VR inputs are optimized to feel responsive and satisfying.

## Walkthrough
-  **Clue #1**: Open the red book on the table
The book guides you to a canvas , i.e the only painting in the zoom so we go there and solve our first part
- **Clue #2** : Swing the canvas frame to find a clue about mirrors and shooting them with pain The room has a mirror, so we pick up the gun right next to it and when we shoot , paint covers the mirror leading us to our next clue.
- **Clue #3** : The mirror tells us about an iconic quote from Julius ceaser along with a 180 degrees
Since the room has a julius ceaser statue , we go there and rotate it
180 to find out next clue
- **Clue #4**: Our final clue tells us about how we should keep the lemons we get in life inside a basket. Looking across the room we see that there is a lemon above our door and a basket on the floor. We grab the lemon and drop it in the basket.

And viola ! The door swings open and we just solved our VR escape room.
